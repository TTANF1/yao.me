The traditional path in frontend has been shaped largely by the technical skills it demands. Before AI reached its current level, building the animations, interactions, visual style, and design language of my personal website would have required learning so much just to realize an idea that it could have felt overwhelming.

Today, AI has greatly shortened that gap. You could even say it has removed the barrier of an idea going unrealized because you lack a particular technical skill—at least in frontend, I think.

Now, having an idea and starting to make it feels almost as simple as picking up a glass of water and taking a drink.

The creator's unusual ideas can come alive on the canvas of a screen. Through different forms of expression, they can convey information to viewers and users, catch their attention, and turn that attention into interest in the content or the product.

That is how I define a new kind of "visual communication" engineer.
